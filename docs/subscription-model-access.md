# Subscription model access

Public subscription pages show provider-published model lineups separately from the smaller set of exact routes admitted for Replay. The October 3, 2026 review adds Kiro Free and the higher Kiro paid tiers, plus Cursor Teams Standard and Premium, while refreshing the current Kiro Pro lineup. These display records do not add executable routes.

Model names without canonical Replay identities remain visible and searchable. They do not acquire API prices, routing aliases or capacity rules merely by appearing here.

- Command Code: separate Go, GOAT, Pro and Max source tables, including 86 named Max models. The lower tiers do not inherit Max's premium lineup.
- ClinePass: all 12 published choices. OpenCode Go/Go Plus: 30 named choices and temporary free-model conditions. Ollama: 17 current cloud choices, separate from local models.
- Claude: current supported models including Sonnet 5.5; Fable remains usage-credit-only on Pro and part of Max's model-specific allowance.
- ChatGPT: Chat and Work/Codex groups; Pro-mode access is not attributed to Plus.
- Copilot: per-plan model matrix, including Sonnet 5.5. Monthly plans exclude retired Sonnet 4.6; GPT-5.4 nano retains its Pro+ Codex-extension restriction. Free uses Auto selection.
- Cursor: individual and Teams plans keep separate per-user Cursor Models and Other Models pools, including Sonnet 5.5. Teams pool sizes are unpublished, and Hobby does not inherit the paid lineup.
- Kiro: paid tiers use the current matrix with Sonnet 5.5 and normal GPT-5.6/Opus 5.5 eligibility. Free has its own smaller named matrix plus Auto. Auto is not assigned one exact identity, and Enterprise-only Fable is excluded.
- Google AI: Gemini Apps, Antigravity and image generation are separate, including Antigravity's Claude and GPT-OSS choices.

Every group links its official source and records the review date. Subscription detail shows a compact searchable lineup; plan search and comparison consume the same public access record. Canonical links use explicit identities only. Current observations are not applied to earlier dates.

Source-of-truth boundaries: `subscription-access-data.json` is reviewed public product information, not an execution catalog. Replay model rules, pricing, allocation and opaque-capacity semantics are unchanged. Kiro individual access is served from its commercial US geography; GPT-5.6 is US-served regardless of enterprise profile region, other profile models follow documented geography, and all availability remains subject to provider geography and account controls. Workspace controls and model retirement can change availability after this snapshot.
