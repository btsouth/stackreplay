Repeated observations of the same unchanged value were not separate candidates. The stored artifacts contain short excerpts and references, not full provider pages.

## Findings, collisions, and later review

Three candidates have no deterministic findings and are `READY_FOR_CATALOG_REVIEW`: Google AI Pro launch price, OpenAI Pro $100 introduction price, and OpenAI Pro $100 standard allowance. This is a human research recommendation only, independent of the M4H-B validator's deterministic findings and candidate review status. Google AI Pro remains ready for *catalog review* after removing the unsupported U.S. condition: the dated provider post establishes the named plan, $19.99 monthly amount, and start date. It is not accepted catalog truth. The original Google AI Ultra price has `unknown_plan` and is `NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE`. Four Anthropic Max candidates and the Pro reset candidate lack historical effective dates and are also `NEEDS_MORE_EVIDENCE`. The two archive candidates have `observed_not_published` and are `REFERENCE_ONLY`; Google AI Plus additionally has `unknown_plan`. No candidate is marked `REJECT` in the selected cohort. Exact findings and full IDs are in the manifest.

Against the accepted catalog version pinned in the manifest, AI Pro $19.99, Pro $100, and Anthropic's current prices and qualitative Max/reset descriptions duplicate current truth while offering potential earlier history. The OpenAI Pro introduction allowance and captured GPT-5.3 rule are new historical information. Original AI Ultra and AI Plus are unmappable to exact current plan IDs. The difference between old Ultra $249.99 and current Ultra 20x $199.99 is **not** an error or an identity match. No `CONFLICTS_WITH_CURRENT_TRUTH` or `SUPERSEDED_MECHANIC` label was assigned to this cohort. Collision labels are human review metadata, not deterministic classifier output.

If separately promoted, the AI Pro and Pro $100 price proposals could add earlier plan versions in `packages/catalog/data/plans/google-ai-pro.yaml` and `packages/catalog/data/plans/openai-chatgpt-pro.yaml`; the listed monthly plan costs are already current accepted values. The OpenAI allowance could add an earlier qualitative statement to the Pro plan. Anthropic candidates would target the exact plan files listed in the manifest, but historical date boundaries are unresolved. Original Ultra, AI Plus, and the GPT-5.3 archive claim have no justified deterministic Replay-impact preview. These are read-only previews, not promotion instructions. M4H-C2 must review exact version intervals, scope, and any Replay consequences before editing accepted YAML.

## Deliberate exclusions

The remaining 2026 OpenAI Plus/Go message counts and generic Pro "unlimited" label need model, fallback, and validity-scope review. LimitWatch's Anthropic 45/225/900-message five-hour figures are explicitly estimates from retired pages and cannot become published numeric limits. Its separate June 15 Anthropic usage-credit entries are promotional funding, not plan capacity. The Google June 8 AI Plus $4.99 email claim was deferred because the public page still showed $7.99 at that snapshot and individual billing start depended on the account; the current page alone cannot supply that historical effective date. Google 5x/20x Ultra multipliers and $99.99/$199.99 current-tier prices were deferred as mostly current duplicates with an unproven June start boundary. Cursor measured pools, legacy GitHub Premium Requests, Replit, xAI and Perplexity community caps, auto-events, news interpretation, usage reports, receipts, subscriber records, and burn data were excluded. No personal or measured dataset was read to construct the cohort.

The candidate JSON and manifest are under `data/catalog-intelligence/m4h-c1`, outside all four accepted catalog YAML directories. The public catalog and Replay do not load this location. Later watchers can generate the same M4H-B candidate shape; LimitWatch references are companion review provenance. Promotion requires a separate reviewed catalog edit, not a candidate status change.
