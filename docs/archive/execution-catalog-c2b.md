# C2B: P0 current execution market expansion

Reviewed on 2026-09-27. This is a manually admitted current-market snapshot. The ten new execution versions and two new direct pricing records activate at `2026-09-27T17:38:00Z`, end exclusively at `2026-10-27T00:00:00Z`, and were compiled at `2026-09-27T17:39:00Z`. The end is a catalog review horizon, not a provider expiration. Provider publication dates and historical effective dates were not inferred from observation or review time. All new accepted versions use `basis: current-market` and `effectiveDateBasis: catalog_activation`, even where a provider has announced an earlier launch. The new prices are USD web or API standard text prices, with the stated unit basis. Account, region, cohort, optional credit, and protocol conditions are never inferred from a provider name.

Accepted authoring remains beside existing plans in `packages/catalog/data/plans`. Material claims carry first-party URL, locator, observation and review time, authority, certainty, normalized claim hash, evidence package hash, and reviewer. These hashes identify reviewed claim metadata rather than storing whole page captures. All new direct routes pin exact pricing refs, endpoint, revision `current-20260927`, USD per million token category rates, and half-open pricing validity. Missing categories are not zero-filled. C2A versions and their artifact hashes remain unchanged. No watcher or automatic admission was added.

## IS THE CATALOG READY FOR PRODUCT UX?

The admitted records are broad enough to show current direct API prices, subscription fixed fees, exact access, purchase/cohort conditions and explicit unknowns across major providers. The representative 900-event workload does **not** yet produce a useful full-workload optimization result. A decision surface can explain known options and missing evidence, but must not present a total cost comparison or purchase recommendation for this workload. The blockers are the missing GPT-5.6 Sol non-promotional route or suitable promotion representation, cache-write TTL evidence for Claude calls, the existing six-subscription candidate bound, and the providers' genuinely opaque quotas. These are recorded limitations, not guessed rates or capacities. C2B changed catalog data and tests only; it did not change O3B.1 optimizer or capacity semantics.
