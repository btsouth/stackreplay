# Published plan decision details

Public subscriptions and Compare distinguish provider-published commercial terms from StackReplay's executable replay constraints. An unimplemented constraint no longer causes a published allowance to disappear or be described as unpublished.

Plan pages put the allowance summary first, followed by searchable model-level tables and practical conditions. Compare shows the same allowance, continuation, privacy and billing facts. Tables preserve source units and conditions; dollar-denominated usage is not a cash balance and cannot be compared across plans without their model rates and multipliers.

OpenCode Go and Go Plus include the full published model allowances, five-hour/weekly fractions, accounting rates and context/peak variants, provider request estimates and assumed token mixes, retention/training differences, client requirements and API identifiers. Missing cache-write rates remain missing. Temporary free models remain conditional. Published request estimates are not fixed quotas. No cross-model balances are summed and no reset anchor is invented.

Each record has a check date and official-source links. These are reviewed snapshots, not a promise that provider terms never change. Current observations are not backdated. Runtime pricing, allocation and capacity semantics are unchanged.
